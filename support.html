Before you dive in

Can I download Gemrift inverse yet?

No public release date or Store listing has been announced here. This website is a preview of the Windows game in development. The original Gemrift is a separate app.

What is different from Gemrift?

Inverse takes Milo underwater: swim through title-themed caves, keep an eye on your air, and use upward-moving rocks to solve the room. The current campaign contains 215 caves.

How do I play?

Use WASD or the arrow keys to move and swim. Hold Space with a direction to snap/dig, press P to pause, and R to retry. Keyboard and controller bindings can be changed in Settings. See the control guide.

Does the clock end my attempt?

The current default is a relaxed clock: reaching zero removes the remaining-time bonus, but play continues. Strict clock is an optional setting. Difficulty also changes the pace and air supply.

What should I include in a bug report?

If you have a development build, include its version, your Windows version, the cave name, what you expected, and the steps to reproduce the problem. A screenshot can help. Do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.
