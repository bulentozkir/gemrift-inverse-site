2. Original 215-cave campaign, Milo Coppercap & music

Every one of the 215 underwater caves in Gemrift inverse is an original level generated from its title and proved in the simulation—none of the caves is imported from GDash or historical level sets.

Milo Coppercap's explorer design, the copper-and-crystal emblem, underwater key art, procedural cave and creature rendering, synthesized creature audio cues, the original 128 BPM chiptune opening composition Pixel Tides, and the native 1920×1111 gameplay screenshots and trailer on this site come directly from the Gemrift inverse project. Additional in-game audio provenance and heritage acknowledgements (including GDash project credits where applicable) are listed in the game's Help → Copyright and authors view.
