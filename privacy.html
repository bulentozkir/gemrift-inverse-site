Gemrift inverse — Underwater Puzzle Adventure

Gemrift inverse and its official website are designed to work without tracking you. Neither this website nor the game requires an account, runs advertising or analytics scripts, or sends gameplay telemetry.

1. A static website without trackers

This website does not use advertising networks, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, or browser databases. All fonts, images, trailer animations, and scripts are served directly with the website rather than loaded from third-party CDNs.

Switching between dark and light appearance or choosing one of the 30 interface languages updates the scoutTheme and lang URL query parameters only; nothing is written to browser storage.

2. GitHub Pages hosting & external links

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load a page, GitHub may process standard technical request metadata—such as your IP address and user agent—for hosting delivery, security, and service reliability under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Following external links—such as the Microsoft Store listing, GitHub repositories, or the original Gemrift site—takes you to services governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Support is completely under your control

The support form assembles a downloadable .eml draft locally inside your browser. It never uploads your message or attachment to a server and never sends email automatically.

If you choose to send an email to the publisher, the publisher receives your email address and any details or attachments you include solely to answer your enquiry or troubleshoot the issue you reported. Please review screenshots and logs to remove private information before sending. Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone and should never contain personal data.

4. Game data & offline play

Gemrift inverse is an offline-playable Windows desktop game. All 215 underwater caves and 430 verified step-by-step JSON solutions are bundled inside the application—playing the campaign or using the in-game Solution Bulb does not connect to external servers or download walkthrough videos.

Your settings, keybindings, completed caves, per-difficulty best scores, and per-cave failed-attempt counters are saved locally on your device in your Windows user profile. If you acquire the game through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes store purchase, installation, and platform services under Microsoft's own privacy statement.

5. Questions & updates

For privacy questions or requests regarding a support email you sent, contact bulentozkir@hotmail.com. Whenever this notice is updated, the effective date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
